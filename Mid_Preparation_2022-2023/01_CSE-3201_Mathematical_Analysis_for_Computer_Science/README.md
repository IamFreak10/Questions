## 📑 Core Syllabus Areas for Mid-Term
1. **Stochastic Processes & Random Variables**:
   - Definition of Stochastic Process $\{X(t), t \in T\}$, state space, parameter space.
   - Classification: Discrete-time vs. Continuous-time, Discrete state space vs. Continuous state space.
   - Stationary processes (Strict-sense stationary vs. Wide-sense stationary) and Evolutionary processes.
   - Random variables: Definition, PMF/PDF, CDF, expectation, variance, and properties.
2. **Special Stochastic Processes**:
   - **Bernoulli Process**: Independent trials, sequence of Bernoulli variables.
   - **Poisson Process**: Definition, inter-arrival time distribution (Exponential), memoryless property.
   - **Martingale Process**: Definition, conditional expectation property $E[X_{n+1} \mid X_1, \dots, X_n] = X_n$.
3. **Probability Distributions & Applications**:
   - Binomial, Geometric, Exponential, and Normal distributions.
