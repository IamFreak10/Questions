### Academic System, Marks Distribution, and Teacher Profiles

## 🏛️ Examination Structure & Marks Distribution

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Barishal follows a standardized grading structure for 3.0 credit theory courses and 1.5/0.75 credit laboratory courses.

### 1. Theory Courses (3.0 Credits) — Total: 100 Marks
- **Continuous Assessment (40 Marks)**:
  - **Midterm Examinations (20 Marks)**: Usually two midterms (1st Mid & 2nd Mid) or one midterm + class tests. Duration: 60 minutes. Typically contains 3–4 questions (e.g., answer 2 out of 3, or all 3 with internal choices).
  - **Class Attendance & Participation (10 Marks)**.
  - **Assignment / Presentation / Class Test (10 Marks)**.
- **Semester Final Examination (60 Marks)**:
  - **Duration**: 3 hours (180 minutes).
  - **Question Structure**:
    - Typically **8 full questions** numbered 1 to 8.
    - Students must **answer any 5 questions** (5 × 12 = 60 marks).
    - In some sessions, the paper is split into **Group A (Modules 1–2)** and **Group B (Modules 3–5)**, where students must answer at least 2 questions from each group.
    - Each 12-mark question is divided into subquestions:
      - Common split: **(a) 4 marks, (b) 4 marks, (c) 4 marks**
      - Or: **(a) 6 marks, (b) 6 marks**
      - Or: **(a) 5 marks, (b) 4 marks, (c) 3 marks**

### 2. Laboratory Courses (1.5 / 0.75 Credits) — Total: 100 Marks
- Continuous evaluation: Lab performance, assignments, viva, attendance (50 Marks).
- Lab Final Examination: Coding task, implementation on hardware/simulator, viva-voce (50 Marks).

## 👨‍🏫 Course Teacher Profiles & Questioning Tendencies

### 1. CSE-3201: Mathematical Analysis for Computer Science
- **Teachers**: Ahsanul Haque, Md. Erfan.
- **Ahsanul Haque's Tendencies**:
  - Emphasizes mathematical proofs and rigorous derivations (unbiasedness of sample variance, Chapman-Kolmogorov equation, first entrance decomposition).
  - Clean question subdivision ((a) definition/concept, (b) proof/derivation, (c) numerical problem).
- **Md. Erfan's Tendencies**:
  - Focuses on real-world applied probability scenarios (e.g., three-way duel, pizza day Markov chain, ML confusion matrix: precision/recall/F1-score).
  - Integrates computer science applications with probability models.

### 2. CSE-3203: Theory of Computation
- **Teacher**: Dr. Md Manjur Ahmed.
- **Tendencies**:
  - Consistent DFA/NFA state transition diagrams and minimization questions.
  - Pumping lemma for regular languages and CFLs is almost guaranteed.
  - High focus on formal definitions of automata tuples \((Q, \Sigma, \delta, q_0, F)\).
  - Turing machine design and undecidability proofs (Halting Problem).

### 3. CSE-3205: Artificial Intelligence & CSE-3207: Peripherals and Interfacing
- **Teacher**: Dr. Tania Islam.
- **AI (CSE-3205) Tendencies**:
  - Heuristic search: A* search graph traversal with table tracking \(g(n), h(n), f(n)\).
  - Game trees: Minimax with Alpha-Beta pruning (asking students to draw cutoffs).
  - Logic: First-Order Logic translation and resolution refutation trees.
- **Peripherals (CSE-3207) Tendencies**:
  - Memory interfacing: Even and Odd memory bank diagrams with \(\overline{\text{BHE}}\) and \(A_0\).
  - 8255 PPI: Control word computation for Mode 0, Mode 1, and BSR mode.
  - Interfacing diagrams: Matrix keyboard, stepper motor sequence, 7-segment display.
  - 8259A PIC priority resolving and cascading diagrams.

### 4. CSE-3209: Simulation and Modeling & CSE-3212: Mobile App Lab
- **Teacher**: Dr. Rahat Hossain Faisal.
- **Simulation (CSE-3209) Tendencies**:
  - Discrete-event simulation table filling for Single-Server Queue (M/M/1) or Inventory models.
  - Random number generators: Linear Congruential Generator (LCG) step calculations.
  - Statistical testing: Runs test (up/down, above/below mean), Chi-Square, and K-S tests.
  - Inverse-transform derivations for exponential and uniform distributions.
- **Mobile App Lab (CSE-3212) Tendencies**:
  - Practical Android architecture: Activity lifecycle state diagrams.
  - Explicit and implicit intent code snippets.
  - SQLite/Room database CRUD operations and RecyclerView adapters.
