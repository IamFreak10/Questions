## 🚀 2. Question Evolution & Style Shift (The 3 Generations)

```
Gen 1 (2016–2018): Direct Definitions & Short Notes
   ├── Define printer & basic technologies
   ├── How does computer mouse work?
   └── Short notes on Hall Effect keyboard

Gen 2 (2018–2020): Technical Metrics, Protocols & Hardware Signals
   ├── Optical mouse accuracy, quality factors, DPI adjustment
   ├── Polling rate vs Refresh rate
   ├── Keyboard matrix scanning, scan time, and key debouncing
   └── Parallel printer Centronics signals (BUSY, PAPER, #ERROR, #INI, #STR)

Gen 3 (2021–2022 — Latest): Real-World Scenario Engineering & Recommendations
   ├── Recommend suitable keyboards for software company roles (Dev, Designer, Data Entry)
   ├── Recommend pointing devices for sterile hospital operating rooms
   └── Recommend heavy-duty printers for high-volume publishing houses
```

## 🎯 7. The New Era: Scenario-Based Engineering Questions

Under Dr. Tania Islam, modern question sets in CSE-3207 end with a 4-mark scenario-based justification question. Mastering these ensures full marks:

1. **Workplace Keyboard Justification (2021–22 Final Q2(c)):**
   - **Programmers:** Mechanical Keyboard (tactile switches, NKRO anti-ghosting, high durability for thousands of keystrokes).
   - **Graphic Designers:** Compact/Tenkeyless (TKL) or Scissor-switch (maximizes desk real estate for drawing tablets, customizable macro keys).
   - **Data-Entry Operators:** Full-size membrane/scissor keyboard with dedicated numeric keypad, quiet keystrokes, wrist rest.

2. **Sterile Environment Pointing Device (2021–22 Final Q3(c)):**
   - **Hospital Operating Rooms:** Fully sealed silicone medical mouse (IP68 certified, waterproof, wipeable with alcohol/bleach), hands-free foot-pedal pointers, or capacitive touchscreens operatable through surgical gloves.

3. **High-Volume Production Printing (2021–22 Final Q4(c)):**
   - **Publishing House (Thousands of pages/day):** Industrial/Enterprise High-Speed Laser Printer or Digital Press (40–70+ PPM, heavy duty cycle 50k–100k+ pages/month, electrophotographic toner with ultra-low per-page consumable cost, zero smudging).
