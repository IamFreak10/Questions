### 1. 

**a)** Explain the need for a peripheral interface in a computer system. Draw a block diagram showing a CPU connected to multiple peripherals through an I/O interface. Describe the basic functions of the interface. Give one advantage of using an interface instead of direct connection.

**b)** Describe how a keyboard is interfaced with a computer. Mention at least two types of keyboards (e.g., membrane, mechanical) and their basic principle difference. Explain the steps from pressing a key to the CPU receiving the data.

**c)** Describe how a mouse is connected to a computer system. Mention at least two types of mice (e.g., optical, mechanical/ball) and the principle of how they detect movement. Explain what types of data the mouse sends to the CPU.
